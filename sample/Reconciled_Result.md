# Reconciled Sample Result

**Result:** Revision Required

The fictional customer notice was reviewed independently by three reviewer roles and then reconciled.

## Reconciled findings

1. **MAJOR — Chronology:** maintenance begins at 10:00 p.m. but is said to finish by 9:00 p.m. the same day.
2. **MODERATE — Date reference:** "next Tuesday" has no issue date, absolute calendar date, or timezone.
3. **MAJOR — Service continuity:** the notice promises uninterrupted service while telling all customers to prepare for two to four hours without internet access.
4. **MAJOR — Pricing:** the upgrade is described as completely free while a mandatory $15 fee is also announced.
5. **MODERATE — Recovery/support:** customers are told to restart once and then wait 48 hours before contacting support, with no useful status or escalation path.
6. **MODERATE — Reliability claim:** the notice guarantees that the upgrade will prevent all future connection problems without supporting evidence.
7. **MINOR — Grammar:** "We is upgrading" is an objective subject-verb agreement error.

## Reconciliation behavior demonstrated

The reconciler also:

- consolidated overlapping reviewer findings;
- separated the relative-date problem from the reversed-time problem;
- adjusted severity when reviewer assessments differed;
- rejected claims that went beyond the evidence;
- retained optional tone/readability improvements without turning them into unsupported blockers;
- preserved a final human decision step rather than approving or publishing automatically.
